
# ✈ AeroMind AI

### Agentic Airline Revenue Intelligence & Network Optimization Platform

AeroMind AI is an enterprise-grade aviation analytics platform designed to simulate modern airline business intelligence systems used by airline network planners, revenue managers, business analysts, and executive leadership teams.

The platform combines route intelligence, revenue analytics, forecasting, and executive decision-support capabilities to help airlines make data-driven operational and strategic decisions.

---

## Executive Overview

AeroMind AI was built to replicate key analytics workflows found in airline intelligence ecosystems such as:

- Sabre Airline Solutions
- Airline Revenue Management Systems
- Network Planning Platforms
- Executive Business Intelligence Suites
- Aviation Operations Command Centers

The platform transforms aviation datasets into actionable business insights through a complete analytics pipeline.
